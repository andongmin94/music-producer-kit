## Mixing remains a separate intervention

When notes may change, consider writing/register/duration alternatives to an unexplained layer conflict.
When the user permits only mixing, preserve notes and choose only verified mix controls; propose rewrites separately.
Describe the intended priority, depth and dynamics before setting parameters. Do not force a fixed chain, frequency cutoff, loudness target or stereo-width curve.
Check clipping, transient behavior and mono compatibility only through actual measurement/listening paths.
Level differences should not automatically decide an A/B preference. Numerical meters do not certify emotion or song quality.

Installed instruments and licensed local samples come first. Do not turn a sonic wish into an automatic purchase or credit spend.
Detailed mix and synthesis evidence remains in the [local library](../../../library/README.md); device implementation still requires actual tool capability verification.
